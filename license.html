Here you can check your license, and ask for paid access after your free trial.

How it works

  • Free trial: 3 days from the first time you open the plugin, on each computer. All presets are included.
  • After the trial: the plugin stops inserting presets, and preset clips already placed in a project are turned off until you unlock. The plugin shows a message to unlock.
  • Paid access: you send payment and a request. I check it and approve it for the time I choose: one month, three months, one year or lifetime.
  • One license is for one computer. It cannot be shared. To move to a new computer, message me.
  • The plugin stores a random computer ID and the date your trial started. No files or personal data are read.
