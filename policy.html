3. License

4. Refunds

  • Digital files cannot be returned, so I do not give refunds after the file has been delivered.
  • If the file is broken, missing or not as described, message me within 7 days. I will fix it or replace it. If I cannot, I will refund you.
  • If I cancel the order before delivery, you will get your money back.

5. Privacy

  • When you order, I collect your name, phone number, email (optional), payment method and TrxID.
